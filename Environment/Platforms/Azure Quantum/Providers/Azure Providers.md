#Cloud #Hardware
[[Azure Quantum]] gives access to $3rd$-party **quantum hardware providers**, each offering real QPUs & simulators through unified API. Providers are added to [[Azure Workspace]] & billed separately according to their own pricing plans. Include: [[Quantinuum]], [[IonQ]], [[PASQAL]], [[Rigetti]].

All providers support Q#, Qiskit, or Cirq circuits submitted through [[Azure Jobs|Azure Quantum jobs]]. Availability by region may vary. All providers can be added/removed from a workspace after creation via the Azure portal or CLI. Provider availability varies by Azure region.
