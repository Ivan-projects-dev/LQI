#Algorithm #Math 
**Hamiltonian encoding** uses the idea that [[Quantum state]] can evolve under Hamiltonian $H(→x)$ that depends on data $→x$. Formally, $1$ prepares init state $|0⟩$ (or another simple reference state) & applies $$U(→x)=e−iH(→x)t$$for some evolution time $t$. 
Hamiltonian $H(→x)$ is constructed so that its params incorporate classical data. Such encodings arise naturally in physics-driven problems or scenarios where time-evolution under data-dependent Hamiltonian is readily implementable.
