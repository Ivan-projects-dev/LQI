#Cybersecurity #Cryptography 
**Code-Based Cryptography (McEliece)** - based on hardness of decoding general linear codes. Known since $1978$, but large key sizes (~1 MB public keys for 128-bit security) made it impractical for most deployments. Classic McEliece was studied as a NIST [[PQC]] candidate but was not selected in the final 2024 round due to key size constraints. Lattice-based schemes (ML-KEM, ML-DSA) & hash-based signatures (SLH-DSA) were selected instead. McEliece remains notable for its long security track record.
